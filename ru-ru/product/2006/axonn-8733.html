Mask of Truth: Kanohi Rode

Allows Axonn to see through any illusion, deception, or shape-shifting disguise utilized by the Piraka on Voya Nui.

The Mighty Guardian of Voya Nui!

Axonn is a powerful, ancient warrior sworn to protect the legendary Mask of Life from those who would use it for evil. Endowed with incredible raw physical strength, his hands can shatter solid bedrock, while his giant double-bladed battle axe channels searing energy blasts capable of stopping an entire Piraka horde in their tracks.

Standing alongside his former comrade Brutaka, Axonn has guarded the secrets of the island of Voya Nui for millennia. But with Brutaka corrupted by the lure of the Ignika, Axonn must now stand alone as the champion of the Matoran and the last barrier against total destruction.

BIONICLE Lore Note:

Axonn’s battle with Brutaka in the 2006 Ignition comic series “Inferno” remains one of the most legendary conflicts in the BIONICLE® universe!
