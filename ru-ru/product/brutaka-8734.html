The Corrupted Titan of the Order of Mata Nui!

Once an honorable guardian and brother-in-arms to Axonn, Brutaka stood watch over the Kanohi Ignika on Voya Nui for millennia. But observing the Great Spirit Mata Nui fall into deep slumber while evil spread across the universe broke his faith. Convinced that the Great Spirit had abandoned creation, Brutaka turned his back on the Order and made a secret pact with the Piraka to seize the Mask of Life for himself.

Brutaka’s towering four-armed form makes him an unstoppable warrior on the battlefield. Equipped with his dual-bladed rotating sword and back-mounted scythes, he easily defeated all six Toa Nuva and six Toa Inika in direct combat! Only his closest comrade, Axonn, possesses the power and determination to stand against him.
