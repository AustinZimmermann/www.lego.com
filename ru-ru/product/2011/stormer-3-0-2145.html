Lead the expedition on jungle planet Quatros!

“Calling Stormer! The evil Witch Doctor has stripped the planet Quatros of its natural Quaza mineral supply, threatening its very collapse! You must lead Bulk, Stringer, Nex, Furno and Rocka to stop him before it is too late.”

Armed with new rhino animal power armor, dual ice ice-blades, and an armored rhinoceros helmet visor, STORMER 3.0 is built to power through dense jungle foliage and withstand any vicious beast under Witch Doctor’s mind control.

Features:

  • Features rhino animal armor plates, white armor pieces, and dual wrist-mounted ice blades.
  • Includes authentic Hero Factory chest core with animal power print.
  • Stands over 6” (17cm) tall in combat readiness!
  • Can be combined with #2143 ROCKA 3.0 to create an ultimate heavy jungle hero!
  • Contains unique code inside canister lid to unlock 300 game points in LEGO Hero Factory online game.
