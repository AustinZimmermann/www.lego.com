Accidentally fired by Hakann, this dark weapon split Vezok into two beings, giving birth to the maniacal Vezon. The spear can fuse living creatures together or rip them apart at will!

The Seventh Piraka & The Beast of Mount Valmai!

Born from an accidental discharge of the Spear of Fusion upon the Piraka Vezok, Vezon is a creature of chaotic cunning, manic humor, and unpredictable ferocity. While his former brethren fought viciously among themselves, Vezon slipped deep into the heart of Mount Valmai to the sacred Chamber of Life.

When he reached for the legendary Kanohi Ignika, the Mask of Life cursed him, fusing the golden mask permanently to the back of his head and enslaving a native giant spider creature, transforming it into the titanic monster Fenrakk. Linked through the power of the mask, any damage dealt to Fenrakk is absorbed and redirected—making them the ultimate guardians of the universe’s most coveted treasure!

“We are two, yet one! And the Mask of Life belongs to me! Try and take it, Toa, and Fenrakk will chew your bones into dust!” — Vezon taunting the Toa Inika in the Chamber of Life.
